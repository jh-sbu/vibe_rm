21 palettes, 5 silent tracks (10/15/30 s). Palettes separate 2–3 layers with
zero IDs; many duplicate silent entries sit between recorded phrases. Durations
include 30/80/120/210/300/600 seconds; fade-outs include 10/12/15 seconds.

and has count 90. Combat tracks have cue points and BNAM outro files.
The tool prints exact metadata for reproducing the survey without exporting
assets. Sources and semantic limits: known_ambiguities/music.md. Public layout:
